FAQs for Monash University Caulfield Campus Australia

What degrees are at Monash Caulfield?

Monash University Caulfield Campus provides a wide range of courses in Business, Arts, Design and Architecture, IT, and Education. The campus is a prime choice for students pursuing creative and professional courses.

What is the acceptance rate of Monash University Caulfield?

The Caulfield campus has an acceptance rate of approximately 40%. It shows the admissions are moderately competitive, and the university is selective.

What does Monash Caulfield specialise in?

Monash University is specialised in Business, IT, Design, Arts, and Science. The campus offers a mix of professional, creativity and innovation learning.

Where do Monash Caulfield students stay?

While Caulfield doesn't have on-campus housing, students live in nearby suburbs like Carnegie, Malvern, or Glen Huntly, with plenty of shared rentals and student accommodation options.

How many people go to Monash Caulfield?

According to Wikipedia, the Monash University Caulfield Campus has over 13,400 students. This includes both domestic and international students, with 57.1% and 52.8% enrolled in UG courses.
